Delete Your Account

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Yapee, Bimaro and Cadaro, published by Onw Dev. You can delete it yourself inside the app, or ask us by email if you can no longer open the app.

Contents

  1. Delete in the app
  2. Ask us by email
  3. What is deleted: Yapee
  4. What is deleted: Bimaro and Cadaro
  5. Plusay and offline apps

1. Delete in the app

  • Yapee: open the Personal tab, choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.
  • Bimaro and Cadaro: open Settings, choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately and signs out every device. It cannot be undone.

2. Ask us by email

Deletion request

  • Send an email to onwdev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  • Include the app name and the email address or username of the account. If you can, send the request from that email address.
  • To protect you, we confirm that the account is yours before we delete it. We complete deletion within 30 days and email you when it is done.

3. What is deleted: Yapee

Deleted

  • Your sign-in details: password hash, email addresses, and linked Google or Microsoft sign-in.
  • Your profile: name, username, avatar, bio and location text. The account is shown as "Deleted user".
  • Your posts, comments and stories.
  • Your Nearby location, push notification devices and AI-character memory.
  • All your sessions. Every device is signed out.
  • Your coin balance and virtual items. They cannot be refunded or transferred.

Kept for a limited time

  • Messages you already sent stay visible to their recipients until they expire from our server (room messages after 3 days, direct and group messages after 7 days). Copies saved on other people's devices are outside our control.
  • Deleted posts and comments are purged from our database after 30 days.
  • Coin purchase and payment records are kept as long as required for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.
  • Reports about abuse are kept for up to 1 year after they are resolved.

4. What is deleted: Bimaro and Cadaro

Deleted

  • Your sign-in details and profile: email address, name, password hash and linked sign-in.
  • All your sessions. Every device is signed out.
  • If you are the last member of your workspace, the workspace and all its projects (Bimaro) or jobs (Cadaro) are deleted too. If other members remain, the shared workspace stays with them.
  • Guest workspaces that never became an account are deleted automatically after 30 days without activity.

Kept for a limited time

  • Payment records are kept as long as required for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase history under Google's policies.

Want to delete only some data? You can delete individual projects, jobs, posts, comments or stories in the app at any time, without deleting your account.

5. Plusay and offline apps

  • Plusay has no accounts. Chats are not stored, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Offline apps and games keep data only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes it.
