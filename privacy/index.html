1. Offline Apps and Games

  • Local data only: offline apps keep progress and settings on your device. We cannot see this data.
  • No personal information: offline apps do not collect your name, email or other personal details.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes its data.

2. Apps with Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Some free games show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device information and ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud.
  • Ads in apps for children are non-personalized and child-directed (see our Children's Privacy Policy).
  • You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In ad-supported games, personalized ads may count as "sharing" under some U.S. state laws: see our U.S. State Privacy Notice.

3. Children

Our kids' games are offline, collect no personal information and show only non-personalized, child-directed ads, if they show ads at all. See our Children's Privacy Policy.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app, and where required we will ask you to accept it before you continue.
