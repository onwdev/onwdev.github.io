1. About Yapee

Yapee is a social app for adults (18+) published by Onw Dev. It offers chat, topic rooms, posts, stories, voice and video calls, games, a "Nearby" feature and virtual coins. This policy explains what information Yapee handles, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • We collect only what Yapee needs to work, keep it safe, and prevent abuse.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Yapee shows no third-party ads.
  • You can delete your account in the app or on our account deletion page.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account: username, display name, email address and password (stored only as a salted Argon2id hash). If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your account identifier, name and email address from that provider.
  • Registration confirmations: that you are 18 or older, and the version and time of the policy you accepted.
  • Profile: optional avatar, bio and location text that you choose to show.
  • Content: messages, photos, reactions, replies, edits, posts, comments, stories, books and chapters, game moves, reports and blocks.
  • Connections: friend requests, contacts and the rooms you join.

Information created when you use Yapee

  • Technical data: session identifiers, IP address, browser or device information and timestamps. We use these to keep you signed in, protect accounts and stop abuse.
  • Presence: whether you are online and which room you have open. This is kept in memory only.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token, the device platform and the app language, so that Firebase Cloud Messaging can deliver them.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, and the call state. Voice and video are carried in real time and are not recorded.
  • Wallet: your coin balance, daily check-ins, top-up orders and virtual item purchases. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach us.

Nearby (optional)

  • Nearby is off until you turn it on. When you share your location, it is rounded to a coarse grid before it is stored, so we never keep your exact position.
  • Other people see only an approximate distance band (for example "1–3 km"), never your location.
  • Sharing ends automatically after the time you choose, and you can stop sharing and erase the stored location at any time.

AI characters

  • Some topic rooms include AI characters. Each one is marked with an "AI" label next to its name and answers honestly when asked whether it is an AI. They never appear as online or in Nearby, and you cannot add them as friends or send them direct messages.
  • They run on simple rules on our own servers. Your messages are not sent to any external AI or language-model provider.
  • We store a small record of how often a character has talked with you. It contains no message text, is deleted after 30 days without interaction, and is removed with your account.
  • You can hide AI characters in any room, or block a character.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account, deliver messages, calls and notifications, show content to the audience you chose, and run games and rooms.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and abuse, investigate reports, and enforce our Terms and Community Rules.
  • To process coin top-ups and purchases, and to answer support and privacy requests.

4. Service Providers

  • Cloud servers that host our databases and APIs.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Google Play: app distribution. A payment provider processes coin top-ups where offered.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: storage and delivery of the photos you send.
  • LiveKit and TURN relay servers: real-time voice and video.
  • Resend: account emails such as verification and password reset.

These providers process information only to provide their service to us, or under their own privacy policies where they act independently, as sign-in and payment providers do. They and our servers may be located in countries other than yours.

5. How Information Is Shared

  • With other users, as you choose: your profile, posts, room messages and stories are visible to their intended audience, and direct messages to their recipients. Anyone who can see your content may save or share it.
  • With service providers, as listed above.
  • For safety and the law: when required by law, or when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect someone from harm, to prevent fraud, or to report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities.
  • Business changes: if Yapee is transferred to a new owner, your information may move with it under this policy.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention and Deletion

Our servers delete old data automatically:

  • Messages: the server keeps a short window: room messages for 3 days (up to the newest 300 per room), and direct and group messages for 7 days (up to the newest 500). Your longer history stays on your own device.
  • Stories last 24 hours. Deleted posts and comments are purged after 30 days.
  • Notifications: 30 days after they are read, and 90 days at most. Call records: 90 days. Finished games: 30 days. Check-in history: 120 days. Resolved reports: 1 year.
  • Sessions, which include IP addresses, are deleted within 30 days after they expire or are revoked.
  • Coin and payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.

When you delete your account, we remove your sign-in details, profile, posts, comments, stories, Nearby location and AI-character memory. Short-lived copies may remain in backups until they are overwritten.

7. Data Security

Keeping your data safe

  • All connections to our servers use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted Argon2 hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. You can sign out your devices from the app.
  • Access to our servers is restricted, and we keep only the data described in this policy.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Children

Yapee is for adults aged 18 and over only. We remove accounts that we believe belong to minors. Report a suspected underage account to onwdev@gmail.com.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. See our Child Safety Standards.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: edit your profile in the app, or ask us for a copy of your personal information.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app (Personal tab → Delete account) or through our deletion page.
  • Controls: block and report people, hide AI characters, turn notifications or Nearby off, and sign out other devices.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours first.

Residents of California and other U.S. states

You have additional rights to know, correct, delete and opt out. See our U.S. State Privacy Notice.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app, and where required we will ask you to accept it before you continue.
