1. About Bimaro

Bimaro is a building modeling and cost estimation app, published by Onw Dev. This policy explains what information Bimaro handles, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • We collect only what Bimaro needs to work, keep it safe, and prevent abuse.
  • Your work belongs to you. We do not sell your personal information or use your data for advertising. Bimaro shows no third-party ads.
  • You can delete your account in the app or on our account deletion page.

2. Information We Collect

  • Guest mode: you can try the app without an account. A temporary guest workspace is created and, if you do not sign up, it is deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Account: email address, name and password (stored only as a salted Argon2 hash), or your Google or Microsoft sign-in identifier, email and name.
  • Workspace: workspace name, country, time zone, language, optional logo and brand color, team members and their roles.
  • Your work: projects, floors, model elements, quantities, estimates, unit prices, snapshots, checklists and reports, plus files you import or export (such as DXF or IFC), which are processed only to provide these features.
  • Billing: your plan, the dates of your trial or Pro pass, and payment records (payment method type, provider reference, amount, currency and status). We never receive your full card number.
  • Technical data: session identifiers, IP address, device or browser information and timestamps, used for sign-in, security and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the tools, save and sync your work across devices, and share it with the teammates you invite.
  • To manage your plan, trial and payments, and to answer support requests.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent fraud and abuse.

4. Service Providers

  • Cloud servers that host our databases and APIs.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Google Play: app distribution and in-app billing in the Android app.
  • Stripe, PayPal or MoMo: payments on the web, where offered.
  • Resend: account emails such as sign-up verification.

These providers process information only to provide their service to us, or under their own privacy policies where they act independently, as sign-in and payment providers do. They and our servers may be located in countries other than yours.

5. How Information Is Shared

  • With your team: the members of your workspace see its shared data.
  • With service providers, as listed above.
  • For safety and the law: when required by law, or to protect people from harm or prevent fraud.
  • Business changes: if Bimaro is transferred to a new owner, your information may move with it under this policy.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Your work is kept while your account or workspace exists. You can delete individual items at any time.
  • Guest workspaces that never become an account are deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Sessions, which include IP addresses, are deleted within 30 days after they expire or are revoked.
  • Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.

When you delete your account, we remove your sign-in details and profile. If you are the last member of your workspace, the workspace and all its data are deleted too. Short-lived copies may remain in backups until they are overwritten.

7. Data Security

Keeping your data safe

  • All connections to our servers use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted Argon2 hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. You can sign out your devices from the app.
  • Access to our servers is restricted, and we keep only the data described in this policy.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Children

Bimaro is a professional tool. It is not directed at children and must not be used by anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required where they live.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: edit your profile and workspace in the app, or ask us for a copy of your personal information.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or through our deletion page.
  • Export: Pro users can export their work in the formats the app offers.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours first.

Residents of California and other U.S. states

You have additional rights to know, correct, delete and opt out. See our U.S. State Privacy Notice.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app, and where required we will ask you to accept it before you continue.
