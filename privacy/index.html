1. Introduction and Scope

Onw Dev ("we", "us") publishes mobile apps and games and runs the online services behind them. This Privacy Policy explains what information each of our apps handles, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to our Android apps, their web versions and this website.

Our apps work in different ways. Offline apps keep everything on your device. Online apps such as Yapee, Bimaro, Cadaro and Plusay need our servers to work. The sections below describe each one so you can see exactly what applies to the app you use.

In short

  • We collect only what an app needs to work, keep it safe, and prevent abuse.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your messages or files to build advertising profiles.
  • You can delete your account inside each online app, or from our account deletion page.

2. Yapee (Social Chat)

Yapee is a social app for adults (18+) with chat, topic rooms, posts, stories, calls, games, a "Nearby" feature and virtual coins.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account: username, display name, email address and password (stored only as a salted Argon2id hash). If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your account identifier, name and email address from that provider.
  • Registration confirmations: that you are 18 or older, and the version and time of the policy you accepted.
  • Profile: optional avatar, bio and location text that you choose to show.
  • Content: messages, photos, reactions, replies, edits, posts, comments, stories, books and chapters, game moves, reports and blocks.
  • Connections: friend requests, contacts and the rooms you join.

2.2 Information created when you use Yapee

  • Technical data: session identifiers, IP address, browser or device information and timestamps. We use these to keep you signed in, protect accounts and stop abuse.
  • Presence: whether you are online and which room you have open. This is kept in memory only, and rooms show a count of the people present.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token, the device platform and the app language, so that Firebase Cloud Messaging can deliver them.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, and the call state. Voice and video are carried in real time and are not recorded.
  • Wallet: your coin balance, daily check-ins, top-up orders and virtual item purchases. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach us.

2.3 Nearby (optional)

  • Nearby is off until you turn it on. When you share your location, it is rounded to a coarse grid before it is stored, so we never keep your exact position.
  • Other people see only an approximate distance band (for example "1–3 km"), never your location.
  • Sharing ends automatically after the time you choose, and you can stop sharing and erase the stored location at any time.

2.4 AI characters

  • Some topic rooms include AI characters. Each one is marked with an "AI" label next to its name and answers honestly when asked whether it is an AI. AI characters never appear as online, in Nearby or in friend suggestions, and you cannot add them as friends or send them direct messages.
  • They run on simple rules on our own servers. Your messages are not sent to any external AI or language-model provider.
  • To keep conversations natural, we store a small record of how often a character has talked with you. It contains no message text, is deleted after 30 days without interaction, and is removed with your account.
  • You can hide AI characters in any room, or block a character.

2.5 How we use Yapee information

  • To create and secure your account, deliver messages, calls and notifications, show content to the audience you chose, and run games and rooms.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and abuse, investigate reports, and enforce our Terms and Community Rules.
  • To process coin top-ups and purchases, and to answer support and privacy requests.

3. Bimaro and Cadaro (Professional Tools)

Bimaro is a building modeling and cost estimation app. Cadaro is a CNC and laser cutting (nesting) app. Both handle information in the same way.

  • Guest mode: you can try the app without an account. A temporary guest workspace is created and, if you don't sign up, it is deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Account: email address, name and password hash, or your Google or Microsoft sign-in identifier, email and name.
  • Workspace: workspace name, country, time zone, language, optional logo and brand color, team members and their roles.
  • Your work:
    • Bimaro: projects, floors, model elements, quantities, estimates, unit prices, snapshots, checklists and reports.
    • Cadaro: jobs, sheets, parts, materials, machine profiles, part libraries and nesting results.
    • Files you import or export, such as DXF or IFC, are processed only to provide these features.
  • Billing: your plan, the dates of your trial or Pro pass, and payment records (payment method type, provider reference, amount, currency and status). In the Android apps, payments go through Google Play. On the web, they go through a payment provider such as Stripe, PayPal or MoMo. We never receive your full card number.
  • Technical data: session identifiers, IP address, device or browser information and timestamps, used for sign-in, security and abuse prevention.

We use this information to provide the tools, sync your work across devices, share projects with the teammates you invite, manage plans and payments, and give support. Your project and job data belongs to you. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Plusay (Random Chat)

  • Plusay pairs anonymous strangers for a chat. It does not require an account, name or email.
  • Matching and messages pass through our server in memory only. Chat messages are not stored in a database and are gone when the chat ends.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse, such as too many connections. Voice and video use real-time connections and are not recorded.

5. Offline Apps, Games and Ads

5.1 Offline apps

  • Local data only: offline apps keep progress and settings on your device. We cannot see this data.
  • No personal information: offline apps do not collect your name, email or other personal details.

5.2 Apps with ads (Google AdMob)

  • Some free games show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device information and ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud.
  • Ads in apps for children are non-personalized and child-directed (see Children's Privacy).
  • You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Yapee, Bimaro, Cadaro and Plusay do not show third-party ads.

6. Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run our services. They process information only on our instructions, or under their own privacy policies where they act independently, as payment and sign-in providers do.

  • Cloud servers that host our databases and APIs.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Google Play: app distribution and in-app billing. Stripe, PayPal or MoMo: web payments, where offered.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: storage and delivery of the photos you send in Yapee.
  • LiveKit and TURN relay servers: real-time voice and video.
  • Resend: account emails such as verification and password reset.
  • Google AdMob: ads in some offline games only.

These providers and our servers may be located in countries other than yours. Wherever your information is processed, we protect it as described in this policy.

7. How Information Is Shared

  • With other users, as you choose: your profile, posts, room messages and stories are visible to their intended audience. Direct messages are visible to their recipients, and project data to the teammates you invite. Anyone who can see your content may save or share it.
  • With service providers, as listed above.
  • For safety and the law: we may disclose information when required by law, or when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect someone from harm, to prevent fraud, or to report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities.
  • Business changes: if an app is transferred to a new owner, your information may move with it under this policy.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, except where AdMob shows personalized ads in our free offline games (see Section 5.2).

8. Retention and Deletion

We keep information only as long as we need it. Our servers delete old data automatically:

  • Yapee messages: the server keeps a short window: room messages for 3 days (up to the newest 300 per room), and direct and group messages for 7 days (up to the newest 500). Your longer history stays on your own device.
  • Yapee stories last 24 hours. Deleted posts and comments are purged after 30 days.
  • Yapee records:
    • Notifications: 30 days after they are read, and 90 days at most.
    • Call records: 90 days.
    • Finished games: 30 days.
    • Check-in history: 120 days.
    • Resolved reports: 1 year.
  • Sessions, which include IP addresses, are deleted within 30 days after they expire or are revoked.
  • Bimaro and Cadaro work is kept while your account or workspace exists. Idle guest workspaces are deleted after 30 days.
  • Payment and coin records are kept as long as needed for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention.

When you delete your account, we remove your sign-in details and profile, and the content described on that page. Short-lived copies may remain in backups until they are overwritten.

9. Data Security

Keeping your data safe

  • All connections to our servers use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted Argon2id hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. You can review your signed-in devices and sign them out.
  • Access to our servers is restricted, and we keep only the data described in this policy.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

Protecting children

  • Yapee is for adults aged 18 and over only. We remove accounts that we believe belong to minors. Report a suspected underage account to onwdev@gmail.com.
  • Bimaro, Cadaro and Plusay are not directed at children and must not be used by anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required where they live.
  • Our kids' games are offline, collect no personal information and show only non-personalized, child-directed ads, if they show ads at all. See our Children's Privacy Policy.
  • We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. See our Child Safety Standards.

11. Your Choices and Rights

11.1 Everyone

  • Access and correction: edit your profile in the app, or ask us for a copy of your personal information.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app or through our deletion page.
  • Controls: block and report people, hide AI characters, turn notifications or Nearby off, and sign out other devices.
  • Ads: reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

We answer requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours first.

11.2 United States residents

Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have additional rights to know, correct, delete and opt out. See our U.S. State Privacy Notice.

We may update this policy as our apps change. We will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app, and where required we will ask you to accept it before you continue.
