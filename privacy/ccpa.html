U.S. State Privacy Notice

California (CCPA/CPRA) and Other U.S. States

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Your rights as a U.S. resident

This notice adds to our Privacy Policy. It is for residents of California and of other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Texas. It applies to Yapee, Bimaro, Cadaro, Plusay and our other apps.

  • Publisher: Onw Dev
  • Contact: onwdev@gmail.com
  • Website: onwdev.github.io

1. Categories of personal information we collect

  • Identifiers: username, display name, email address, account and device identifiers, push tokens, IP address, and the advertising ID in ad-supported games.
  • Customer records: account details, workspace details, and payment records such as plan, amount and provider reference. We never receive full card numbers.
  • Commercial information: coin top-ups, virtual item purchases, and Pro passes and trials.
  • Internet or network activity: sessions, sign-in times, device and browser type, and in-app activity needed to run the features.
  • Approximate geolocation: only if you turn on Yapee Nearby. It is rounded to a coarse grid before storage.
  • Audio and visual information: photos you send. Calls are not recorded.
  • Professional information: projects, models, estimates and cutting jobs you create in Bimaro and Cadaro.
  • Sensitive personal information: your account password, which we store only as a hash. We do not use sensitive information to infer characteristics about you.

Sources: you, your device, the sign-in and payment providers you choose, and other users (for example, when they message you or invite you to a project). Purposes and retention periods are described in each app's privacy policy: Yapee, Bimaro, Cadaro and Plusay.

2. Selling and sharing

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Yapee, Bimaro, Cadaro and Plusay do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • In free games that use Google AdMob, personalized ads may count as "sharing" under California law. You can opt out by turning off personalized ads, or deleting your advertising ID, in your device settings. You can also email us with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share".
  • We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

3. Your rights

  • Right to know and access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete. You can do this yourself in the app or through our deletion page.
  • Right to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We already use it only to provide the service.
  • Right to non-discrimination for using these rights.
  • Right to appeal a decision about your request, where your state provides one.

4. How to make a request

  • Email onwdev@gmail.com with the subject "US Privacy Request". Include the app name and the email address or username on the account.
  • We confirm requests within 10 business days and answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this as the law allows and will tell you.
  • We verify requests by checking control of the account email. An authorized agent may make a request with your signed permission.
