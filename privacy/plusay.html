1. About Plusay

Plusay is an anonymous random chat app published by Onw Dev. It pairs you with a stranger for a text, voice or video chat. This policy explains what information Plusay handles.

In short

  • No account, name or email is needed.
  • Chats are not stored: they pass through our server in memory and are gone when the chat ends.
  • Calls are not recorded. Plusay shows no third-party ads.

2. Information We Handle

  • Chat messages: relayed through our server in memory only, to deliver them to your partner. They are not written to a database.
  • Connection data: your IP address and a temporary session identifier, used while you are connected and to limit abuse (for example, too many connections from one address).
  • Voice and video: carried in real time between the two devices, directly or through a relay server when your network requires it. They are not recorded.
  • Optional matching preferences you choose in the app, kept only for the current session.

3. How We Use Information

  • To match you with a partner and deliver your messages and calls.
  • To prevent spam and abuse, for example by limiting the number of connections from one address.

4. Service Providers

  • Cloud servers that run the matching and relay service.
  • TURN relay servers that carry voice and video when a direct connection is not possible.

5. How Information Is Shared

  • Your messages and video go only to the partner you are chatting with. Your partner may capture what they see, so do not share personal information.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, or to protect someone from harm.
  • We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

Chats and session data are kept in memory only while the chat lasts, and abuse-limiting counters for a short time after. Nothing is kept once they expire.

7. Data Security

Keeping your data safe

  • Connections to our servers are encrypted.
  • We store no chat history, so there is no history to leak.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Never share passwords, payment details or other private information with strangers.

8. Children

Plusay is not directed at children and must not be used by anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required where they live.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. See our Child Safety Standards.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can end a chat or skip a partner at any time. Because Plusay keeps no account or chat history, there is no stored personal data to access or delete. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your device.

Residents of California and other U.S. states

You have additional rights to know, correct, delete and opt out. See our U.S. State Privacy Notice.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app, and where required we will ask you to accept it before you continue.
