UK & EEA Notice

Availability of Onw Dev Apps

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Our apps are not offered in the UK or EEA

Onw Dev's apps and online services are offered to users outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area (EEA). The EEA is the 27 EU member states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway. Our apps are not distributed in these countries on Google Play.

  • Publisher: Onw Dev
  • Contact: onwdev@gmail.com
  • Website: onwdev.github.io

1. What this means for you

  • Our apps are not available on Google Play in the UK or EEA, and they are not marketed to people there.
  • If you use one of our apps there anyway, for example after installing it elsewhere or while travelling, our Privacy Policy and Terms still describe how it works. You can delete your account at any time.
  • Offline apps keep data only on your device. Uninstalling them removes that data.

2. If your information reaches us

If you are in the UK or EEA and believe we hold your personal information, email us. Whatever the reason, we will:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct it, or delete it together with any account;
  • stop any further processing;
  • reply within 30 days.

Please use the subject "UK/EEA Request" and include the app name and the email or username on the account.

3. Consumer information

Because our apps are not distributed in the UK or EEA, our Google Play developer profile is declared accordingly, and Google Play shows the related consumer information there. For everyone else, our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

4. Contact

  • Email: onwdev@gmail.com
  • Subject: "UK/EEA Request"
  • Response: within 30 days
