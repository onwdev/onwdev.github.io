Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

These standards apply to Yapee, Plusay and every other Onw Dev app where people can communicate or share content. Onw Dev has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking.

1. Prohibited Content and Behavior

  • Any content that sexualizes minors, including drawn, AI-generated or "fictional" depictions.
  • Grooming: building trust with a minor in order to exploit them, asking a minor for images, or arranging to meet a minor.
  • Sextortion, sharing or requesting CSAM, and any link to such material.
  • Any other content or conduct that puts a child's safety at risk.

2. How We Prevent Harm

  • Adults only: Yapee is for people aged 18 and over. Every user must confirm their age when registering, and we remove accounts that we believe belong to minors.
  • Safe defaults: direct messages require an accepted friend request. Nearby is off by default, and it shows only approximate distance bands, never exact locations.
  • Tools for everyone: you can block any user and report users, messages, posts and rooms from inside the app.
  • AI characters are labelled as AI, never send private messages, and point people at risk to real help.

3. Reporting

How to report

  • In the app: use Report on the user, message, post or room.
  • By email: write to onwdev@gmail.com with the subject "Child Safety Report". Include the app name, the username and a description. Do not attach abusive images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number first.

4. How We Respond

  • We review child-safety reports as a top priority.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the competent authorities, and we preserve the evidence they may need.
  • We cooperate with valid law-enforcement requests.

5. Compliance

We follow Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and the child-protection laws that apply to our services. These standards are part of our Terms of Service, and breaking them ends your access to all our apps.
